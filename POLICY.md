## 1. Subscription Policy

### 1.1 Subscription Plans

- Each plan has specific constraints including view limits, post limits, and duration

### 1.2 Subscription Replacement & Overwrite Policy

**⚠️ CRITICAL POLICY - READ CAREFULLY:**

- **Subscription Overwrite:** When you purchase a new subscription while having an active subscription:
  - Your current/active subscription will be **immediately replaced** with the new subscription
  - This action is **irreversible** and **cannot be undone**
  - **No refunds** will be issued for any remaining time on your current subscription
  - All unused features, views, or credits from your current subscription will be **forfeited**
  - The new subscription starts immediately upon successful payment

- **No Partial Refunds:** Users will not receive:
  - Prorated refunds for unused days
  - Credit transfers from old to new subscription
  - Compensation for lost features or views

- **User Responsibility:** By proceeding with a new subscription purchase while having an active subscription:
  - You acknowledge that you understand this policy
  - You accept full responsibility for the replacement of your current subscription
  - You waive any right to dispute or request refunds for the overwritten subscription

### 1.3 Payment Processing
- Payments are processed through MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money
- Currency conversion applies: XAF amounts are converted to EUR for MTN sandbox (1 EUR ≈ 655.957 XAF)
- Payment confirmation is required via mobile money PIN entry
- Transactions are tracked and recorded in our system with unique transaction IDs

- **No Refunds:** All subscription payments are **non-refundable**

  - Misunderstanding of plan features

- **Exceptions:** Refunds may be considered ONLY in cases of:
  - Duplicate charges due to system errors (verified by our team)
